Common questions

Can you operate a plant we already own?

Yes. We provide the crew, supervision and maintenance, and report output against agreed targets.

Which aggregate sizes do you supply?

20 mm, 10 mm, crushed sand and stone dust as standard. Other grades are made to order.

Do you provide test certificates?

Yes. Grading and strength results are recorded for each batch and shared on request.
