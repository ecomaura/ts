What we do

Three service lines, each run by people who work on site every day.

Mining engineering

Technical planning that keeps a mine safe, legal and productive.

  • Mine planning, bench design and scheduling
  • Drilling and blasting design
  • Surveying, reserve estimation and reporting
  • Safety, environment and statutory compliance support

Crusher plant operation & maintenance

We take over your plant, or support your own crew, and hold it to output and uptime targets.

  • Full plant operation with trained crews
  • Preventive and breakdown maintenance
  • Liner, screen and conveyor belt replacement
  • Spares management and performance reporting

Aggregates & concrete supply

Consistent grading, tested mixes, and delivery on the schedule your site runs to.

  • Graded aggregates, sand and stone dust
  • Ready-mix concrete in standard and project grades
  • Batch-wise quality testing and test certificates
  • Bulk delivery to site
